Desportivos ’26

The sports fest of LNMIIT Jaipur. The 2026 edition ran on a Pixel Paradox theme, and everything below is built from that one idea.

Role
Graphic designer
For
LNMIIT Jaipur
Year
2026
Scope
Logo, mascot, rollout, merch

Logo unit

The theme was 8-bit pixelated, inspired by retro gaming visuals. I designed the logo unit with a bold, block-based style influenced by Minecraft, using chunky, stone-textured letterforms to reflect strength, competition, and the energetic spirit of the sports fest.

Mascot

From pencil sketch, to line art redrawn in Illustrator, to the final coloured character. The headband and spiky hair reinforce the dynamic, action-driven theme; a confident stance and subtle smirk give him a bold yet approachable personality.

Alternate

This alternate version amplifies energy and attitude. The exaggerated grin, expressive eyes, and crossed-arm stance create a confident, competitive vibe.

Banners and posters

The rollout: main announcement, Inceptio, the countdown, event banners, a pixel-art campus map and the stage banner.

Merch

The back print blends modern character illustration with classic 8-bit visuals; a low-angle perspective makes the mascot feel powerful. Blue adds balance and contrast, and the front keeps it quiet with a single small mark so the back carries the weight.
