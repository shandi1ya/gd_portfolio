Vignette

The art and design club of LNMIIT Jaipur. The old mark was illustrative and generic; the new one is a single symbol that survives at any size.

Role
Rebrand
For
Vignette, LNMIIT Jaipur
Scope
Logo, mascot, merch

Before

  • Fine details could get lost in smaller applications
  • Generic and common art symbols, reduces uniqueness
  • More illustrative than iconic, affecting memorability
  • Reduced clarity due to smaller elements

After

  • Minimal and iconic, making it instantly recognizable and memorable
  • Strong, bold outlines improve visibility and scalability across digital and print mediums
  • Feels more symbolic than literal, giving the club a stronger identity rather than just representing “art tools”

Lockups

Primary and neutral horizontal lockups.

Mascot

The octopus represents versatility and multi-disciplinary creativity, its arms standing for the many forms of art practiced in the club. The blue palette aligns with the logo, and the illustrative personality adapts across posters, merchandise, social media and event branding.

Merch

Bold graffiti typography and spray-paint elements on the front; comic panels on the back, with blue from the club’s logo reinforcing the identity.
