Ode

Brand Identity and Graphic Design Intern at Eve, working under the founder Ms. Aditi Sharma. The brief: turn Ode’s philosophy, because plans need people, into things you can actually hold.

Role
Brand identity intern
For
Ode at Eve
Year
Summer 2026
Scope
Print, merch

The wordmark

My core responsibility was to help translate Ode’s brand philosophy into physical design products: flyers, tote bags, t-shirts. Each piece ran from brief to final artwork under regular supervision.

Identity

The system I worked inside: lockups, palette and type voice.

Flyers

The flyers had to explain in a single glance what Ode is: tagline, a clear description of the app and a QR code for download. Made for physical distribution at events.

Business card

Designed for Ms. Aditi as founder: a premium, minimal identity carried in the simplest physical format there is.

Bookmarks

Low-cost promotional material people would actually keep rather than discard, unlike a typical flyer or handout. Seven variants, each built to be remembered.

Tote bags

Everyday merchandise that carries the brand into ordinary public space and college campuses.

T-shirts

The same editorial, typography-first approach extended into wearable fashion merchandise, aimed at a design-conscious student audience.

Badges & tumblers

Badges built around typography and short, confident lines rather than illustration. Tumblers treated as premium everyday merchandise rather than a one-off event handout.

Monthly planner

A functional, everyday object that keeps the brand present in someone’s routine long after any single event.
